**Goal:** add income besides BidBrief and HaulProof, with work that helps people, using what the owner already has:
- an LLC (Mindset Logistics LLC, Dallas)
- a cargo van
- an active USDOT number, 2797403 (the MC authority is not active)
- Claude to build software

The pay figures come from job boards and vendor sites. Treat them as ranges, not promises.

## 1. Cargo van courier routes

- Dallas contractor routes list **$700–$1,800/week**, and dedicated routes **$150–$225/day**. Examples include MD Courier Service (Walmart, $200–$225/day), RS Logistics, K.D.L Global Xpress, TForce Logistics and Courier Direct.
- **Higher margin, later:** contract directly with local pharmacies, labs and clinics instead of through middlemen.
- **Needs:**
  - commercial auto insurance (and cargo insurance for some routes)
  - a van under about 10 years old
  - the LLC's EIN for the W-9
- **No MC authority is needed for work that stays inside Texas.**

## 2. Junk removal and small hauling

- Dallas small-load pricing is about **$79–$155**. One DFW operator charges **$99** for a single item, **$149** for a small load, **$275** for a medium and **$475** for a large load.
- The van is already paid for, which saves most of the usual $5K–$30K startup.
- **Get customers with:** a free Google Business Profile, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace and apartment managers.
- **Important:** moving people's *household goods* for pay in Texas requires a **TxDMV number and cargo insurance, whatever the vehicle size**. Junk removal and hauling items to the dump or donation centers is different. Keep to junk and haul-away unless you register with TxDMV.

## 3. Texas notary and loan signing agent

- Loan signings pay **$75–$200 each** (purchases and refinances about $100–$200).
- **To get the commission:**
  - a 4-year commission
  - a **$10,000 surety bond** (the premium is small)
  - a **state-required 2-hour course and a 20-question test**, required since Jan 1, 2026
- Signing-agent training and E&O insurance are optional but expected by title companies.
- Fits evenings and weekends, and the van makes it a mobile service.
- Check the current fee caps on the Texas Secretary of State site before quoting prices.

## 4. Small-carrier compliance helper (software plus service)

- **Why:** the owner just went through this. The MCS-150 was overdue, and Motus's "Link USDOT Number" didn't work. Thousands of small carriers are stuck on the same thing after the May 2026 Motus switch.
- **What to build:**
  - reminders for MCS-150, UCR (window Oct 1–Dec 31), insurance renewals and authority status
  - plain-English "how to fix it" guides
  - an optional done-with-you filing help service
- **Competitors:** filing services charge about **$175 per MCS-150**, and Fiverr sellers offer BOC-3/UCR/MCS-150 packages.
- **Fit:** this is the **Compliance Tracker add-on already on the HaulProof roadmap**. HaulProof's new-carrier lead finder supplies the customers.
- **Rules:**
  - never imply FMCSA affiliation
  - always say the filing itself is free from FMCSA
  - don't act as a BOC-3 process agent (that needs agents in every state); partner with one instead

## 5. Win small government contracts as Mindset Logistics LLC

- Register the LLC on SAM.gov (free) under courier and delivery NAICS codes.
- Use BidBrief to find **courier, delivery and moving** orders in DFW.
- Small buys (micro-purchases, up to about $10K–$15K) are the easiest first wins.
- Each win is also a true BidBrief success story for marketing.
- Contact the Dallas APEX Accelerator (free help) and the small business offices at local VA and DoD sites.

## 6. Affiliate income on HaulProof

- "Sponsored" links on free carrier pages for factoring, truck insurance, ELDs and fuel cards. Already on the HaulProof roadmap. Low effort once traffic grows.

## First 30 days

1. **This week:** apply to 5–10 courier routes. Get a commercial auto insurance quote. Set up a free Google Business Profile for "Mindset Logistics: junk removal & hauling, Dallas."
2. **Week 2:** start the Texas notary course and application.
3. **Week 2–3:** finish the MCS-150 in Motus (call FMCSA at 1-800-832-5660 if linking still fails). Register the LLC on SAM.gov.
4. **Week 3–4:** Claude builds the HaulProof Compliance Tracker: MCS-150, UCR and insurance reminders, plus a Motus how-to guide.
