# BidBrief: First Google Ads Plan

**Budget:** $10 a day (about $300 a month). Run it for 3 weeks, then review.
**Goal:** free sign-ups and free bid checks, not clicks.

---

## Account settings (same for the whole campaign)

| Setting | Choose |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | **Search** (not Performance Max) |
| Goal | Website traffic (switch to "Conversions" once tracking is added) |
| Networks | **Uncheck** "Search partners" and "Display Network" |
| Locations | United States, "Presence: people **in** this location" |
| Language | English |
| Bidding | **Maximize clicks**, max cost per click **$3.00** |
| Daily budget | **$10** |
| AI options | **Turn off** "auto-apply recommendations" and "broad match" |

---

## After you launch

- **Weeks 1–3:** don't change anything for the first 7 days. Then once a week:
  - In **Keywords → Search terms**, add anything off-topic to the negative list.
  - Pause keywords that have had 30+ clicks and no sign-ups.
- **Tracking sign-ups:** send Claude your Google Ads tag ID (starts with `AW-`, safe to share). Claude adds it to BidBrief so Google can see which ads bring sign-ups. Then switch bidding to **Maximize conversions**.
- **More trades later:** copy ad group 2 and change "janitorial" to `trucking`, `security-guard`, `hvac`, `roofing`, `moving` and so on. The page is `/government-contracts/<trade>`.
